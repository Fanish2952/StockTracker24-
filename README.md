# StockTracker24'-
The Stock Tracker App provides real-time stock updates and news, keeping users informed without the ability to buy or sell stocks.

**Stock Tracker App Description:**

I am developing a user-friendly mobile application called Stock Tracker App. This app is designed to provide users with real-time updates and comprehensive information about any stock they are interested in. While users cannot buy or sell stocks through the app, they can stay informed with detailed stock performance updates, price trends, and historical data. Additionally, the app curates the latest news and articles about each stock, keeping users updated on market developments, company updates, and industry trends.

The Stock Tracker App is perfect for market enthusiasts, beginners learning about stocks, or anyone who wants to monitor their favorite stocks without engaging in trading. The goal is to create a reliable and convenient platform to help users make informed decisions and stay updated on the stock market.
